2Cast & company search — free, no account

TV shows search TVMaze (full cast + real per-episode guest stars, with photos), falling back to Wikidata, then a Wikipedia infobox read for names only. This also covers webisode-style talk shows and interview podcasts now on streaming (Drink Champs, Truth or Drink, and the like) — hosts and guests appear under their own name since there's no fictional character to list. Give a season number with no episode number to pull per-episode guest tracking for a whole season at once. Movies search Wikidata (cast + character + actor photo), with the same Wikipedia fallback for titles too obscure to have structured cast data. Company/network/studio adds a production company, distributor, or channel — e.g. "Filmrise" — so a logo bumper or ident is identifiable in the pool too. Free one-click manual search links (Google included) appear below for anything none of these turn up. None of this needs a key or sign-up.

3Speaker pool — cast lineup

Each card is a reference face — or logo — from TVMaze, Wikidata, or a photo you attach yourself. Add a production company, network, or distributor here too (e.g. "Filmrise") to help identify a bumper or ident on screen; a logo won't get face-matched automatically, but it's there for a human to recognize while scrubbing. On-screen matches populate the filmstrip underneath and feed the voice map automatically. A name added with no photo (e.g. from the Wikipedia fallback) can't be face-matched until one is found — use "Find photos" above, or attach one by hand on the card.
