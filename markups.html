Runs entirely in your browser — scene-cut and fade detection is deterministic frame-difference analysis (not AI), cross-referenced with quiet audio moments. No upload, no network call.
What this actually does: finds real scene cuts and fades, then picks the best-scoring one inside each target time window from the Delivery Guide's spacing rules. It won't know a cut is mid-dialogue-continuation vs. a true scene change — quiet-audio cross-referencing helps but isn't perfect. Every suggestion needs a human check before it's treated as final, same as every other flagged item in this tool set. Only US Film and US TV (30/60 min) rules are fully encoded from the Delivery Guide; other regions use the Generic custom-spacing mode — reference the guide's own table for your region's target segment length and enter it there.
