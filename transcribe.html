Models — try any combination, compare

⚡ Fast

Whisper tiny — quick, rougher. Good for a first pass or a long file.

~40MB one-time download, cached after that

🎧 Small

Whisper small — a genuine middle ground between Fast and Accurate, worth trying on dialogue-heavy or noisier audio.

~250MB one-time download, cached after that — noticeably slower to load than Fast/Accurate

🎯 Accurate

Whisper base — slower, noticeably cleaner output.

~80MB one-time download, cached after that
All three models run entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded except the one-time model download from Hugging Face's model hub. Output is a first draft: open the exported .vtt in Analyze to fix speaker IDs, formatting, and anything the model got wrong.
