Scope, read before trusting this for delivery: supports pop-on captions only (the standard for pre-authored, non-live content) on a single channel (CC1/CC2). SCC's grid is a fixed 15 rows × 32 columns — VTT/SRT positioning and styling get quantized to fit it, so this is not a byte-identical round-trip in that direction. Roll-up and paint-on caption styles are not supported. Validate the output against a real decoder (Ooona, a broadcast QC tool) before delivery — this has not been tested against a hardware/broadcast decoder.
Built from directly-verified logic in Eyevinn/go-608 (MIT licensed) — character tables, control codes, and drop-frame timecode math ported from its source rather than reconstructed from memory.
